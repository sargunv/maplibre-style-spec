Ground widths are estimates by road class: a local street is 7 m wide. Road casings, bridges, tunnels and paths scale together. These are styling choices, not surveyed road widths.

Drag or zoom either map; the other follows. City changes keep the zoom. Ground sizing approximates scale near the center on flat Mercator; pitch, terrain and latitude variation across the view need more than this input.

How this works & source

The proposed ["latitude"] expression reads map-center latitude inside the renderer. The page supplies no latitude state to the style. Base-2 zoom interpolation and 1 / cos(latitude) turn a ground length into pixels.
